About Me

kristianwingo@gmail.com @k_wingo

Who, me? You wanna know about me?

That depends on who's asking I suppose.

Jobswise, I'm an education man. I got my start teaching my native tongue, English most precisely. Did that clear on t'other side of the world, in a little town called Seoul. Taught kids, elementary and middle schoolers. Came back stateside to do some more learnin myself. Got me a master's degree in the thing I'd been doin for a year an a half. Did that thing again (teachin English if you remember) except this time on college campuses in Illinois and Missouri.

Had to take a detour into university administration for a time on account of a precipitous loss in English learner enrollment. Well no matter. I got me yet another Master's degree, this time in they were callin a hot field: educational technology. Folks go gaga over anything tech, don't they? Basically I learned how to push around digital doodads so that folks can learn stuff better while lookin at screens, if that makes any darn sense. After finishin up that master's, I got me a job designing instruction, which basically means I helped college professors spruce up their online courses, makin sure they're followin all the right principles of pedagogy, accessibility, and what not. Did that a couple years at a university, and then switched over to a consulting firm, helpin out government clients digitally upskill their own employees a bit better. Then I grabbed my tech skills and hopped back into the language learnin world again when i got a job they call language technology specialist at a little institution of higher education called Yale. It's a fancy title, but basically i help language faculty with technology. And that's what I'm still doin now, given talks on AI, helpin folks set up their online textbooks, and basically just usin everything i've learned up til this point.

Anyway, that's about what I've been doin jobswise.

Now, hobbieswise, I'm a film guy. Movies, more colloquially. I like writin em, directin em, heck I like screwin around and actin in em too if the mood strikes me. I got me a good little community here, call it Hang Out Films cuz that's what we do: hang out and film. We gather up and do some workshops, some festivals, some productions. See, makin movies is a dream of mine that I'm livin, and basically I'm just tryin to make sure as many other folks as possible can live that dream too. Cuz that's what life's all about, ain't it?
